## 🌟 Overview

**Türkiye Bursları** is the Turkish Government's flagship scholarship for international students. It is run by the **Presidency for Turks Abroad and Related Communities (YTB)** and covers study at public universities in Türkiye.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Provider** | Government of Türkiye (YTB) |
| **Levels** | Associate, Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, and research scholarships |
| **Open to** | International students from most countries |
| **Application system** | Online (TBBS, the Türkiye Scholarships application system) |
| **Cost to apply** | Free |
| **Language** | Turkish or English programmes. A Turkish language course is usually included |

---

## 🎓 Programme Levels

| Level | Typical duration | Notes |
|-------|------------------|-------|
| **Associate / Bachelor's** | About 4 years (plus 1 year of Turkish language where needed) | For secondary school graduates |
| **Master's** | 2 to 3 years | Needs a bachelor's degree |
| **PhD** | About 4 years | Needs a master's degree |
| **Research scholarships** | Short-term | For researchers (separate rules) |

---

## ✅ Who Can Apply

Common conditions. The official guide for the year is the final authority.

- **Nationality:** Citizen of an eligible country (not a Turkish citizen).
- **Age limits (reported):** Bachelor's under about 21, Master's under about 30, PhD under about 35. Research programmes have higher limits. Check the current rule.
- **Grades (reported):** Minimum grade levels apply. A common figure is around 75% for master's and PhD, with higher requirements for some health and basic science fields. Check the current rule.
- **Education:** Completed the previous degree level required (secondary school for bachelor's, bachelor's for master's, master's for PhD).
- **One application per cycle:** Multiple applications can lead to disqualification.
- **Not studying on another scholarship in Türkiye,** and generally not a previous Türkiye Bursları recipient.
- **Language:** Often no IELTS or TOEFL is required to apply, but programme-specific language rules apply.

## 💰 Benefits

Amounts change. Reported monthly stipend figures differed between sources, so confirm on the official site.

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Tuition** | Fully covered |
| **Accommodation** | Dormitory housing or accommodation support |
| **Monthly stipend** | Paid in Turkish lira (amounts vary by level and year) |
| **Health insurance** | Included |
| **Airfare** | Round-trip ticket |
| **Turkish language course** | Typically 1 year where required |
| **Additional support** | Orientation and student support activities |

## 📅 Typical Timeline

| Period | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **10 Jan – 20 Feb** | Online applications (standing calendar) |
| **Mar – Apr** | Preliminary evaluation |
| **Apr – Jun** | Interviews for shortlisted candidates |
| **Early Aug** | Results announced |
| **Aug – Sep** | Preparations for visa and travel |
| **Sep – Oct** | Arrive in Türkiye, language course, and start |

> The full path from application to first class can take a long time for some levels. Plan ahead.

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application

1. **Read the official guide** on the Türkiye Bursları website.
2. **Check eligibility** (age, grades, level, and country).
3. **Choose up to your allowed number of university and programme preferences** as listed in the form.
4. **Create an account** on the application system.
5. **Fill in the application** and upload documents.
6. **Submit before the deadline.** Keep your confirmation.
7. **Wait for the preliminary result,** then prepare for the interview if shortlisted.
8. **Attend the interview** (online or in person as instructed).
9. **Receive the final result** and complete visa and travel procedures.

## 📂 Documents Checklist

Check the official list for the year.

- [ ] Passport or national ID
- [ ] Photo
- [ ] Transcripts of the latest completed degree
- [ ] Certificate of graduation (or expected graduation)
- [ ] Grade conversion information (if needed)
- [ ] Reference letters or recommendation (for graduate levels, if requested)
- [ ] Research proposal (for PhD or research, if required)
- [ ] Language certificates (if you have them and they are accepted)
- [ ] Awards and certificates (optional)

## 🏫 Choosing Universities and Programmes

1. Review the **list of universities and programmes** open to scholarship students.
2. Check which programmes are in **Turkish** and which are in **English**.
3. Choose programmes that match your degree, grades, and goals.
4. Use all your preference slots wisely, with a mix of competitive and safe options.
5. Remember that placement depends on the evaluation and available quotas.

## 💡 Tips

- **Prepare early,** because the window is only about six weeks.
- **Submit only one application.**
- **Scan clear, correct documents** and keep file sizes within limits.
- **Choose your preferences carefully** and read about each university.
- **Prepare for the interview** with a clear explanation of your goals and why Türkiye.
- **Learn basic Turkish.** It helps in the interview and daily life.
- **Check official announcements** instead of relying on social media.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Submitting more than one application
- Missing age or grade requirements
- Blurry or incorrect document scans
- Waiting until the last days of the window
- Relying on unofficial pages for dates and amounts
- Ignoring the interview preparation

---

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- Türkiye Bursları is **free to apply**. There is no application fee.
- Be careful with agents or pages that promise guaranteed selection for money.
- Use only the official Türkiye Bursları website and YTB channels.
