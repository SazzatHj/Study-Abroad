## 🌟 Overview

**Chevening** is the UK Government's international scholarships programme, funded by the **Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO)** and partner organisations. It supports future leaders from eligible countries to study a **one-year master's degree** at a UK university.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Provider** | UK Government (FCDO) and partners |
| **Level** | One-year taught master's degree |
| **Eligible countries** | 160+ countries and territories |
| **Selection focus** | Leadership potential, influencing and networking skills, academic ability, and career plan |
| **Application** | Online, at chevening.org |
| **Tracks** | Standard Chevening, plus special tracks such as Small Island Developing States and ASEAN (country rules apply) |

---

## ✅ Who Can Apply

These are common conditions. The official eligibility page for your country is the final authority.

- **Citizenship:** Citizen of a Chevening-eligible country or territory.
- **Return home:** Willing to return to your home country for at least **two years** after the scholarship ends.
- **Degree:** An undergraduate degree that qualifies you for a UK master's programme.
- **Work experience:** Commonly **at least two years (about 2,800 hours)** of work experience, which can include paid work, internships, volunteering, or unpaid roles.
- **Courses:** Apply to **three eligible UK master's courses** and secure at least one **unconditional offer** by the deadline.
- **Previous awards:** You must not have previously received a Chevening Scholarship or other UK government-funded scholarship (check the rules for exceptions).
- **Other restrictions:** Not a British or dual British citizen, and not working for certain UK government bodies (see the official list).

## 📂 Application Components

- [ ] Personal details and eligibility answers
- [ ] **Four essays** (see below)
- [ ] **Three course choices** (UK master's, one-year)
- [ ] **Two references** (academic or professional)
- [ ] Details of your education and work experience
- [ ] English language evidence, if required by your chosen universities
- [ ] Supporting documents requested if you are shortlisted

---

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application

1. **Check the Chevening page for your country** and confirm eligibility.
2. **Research UK courses** that match your career plan (one-year master's only).
3. **Select three courses** from different universities where possible.
4. **Write the four essays** and ask someone to review them.
5. **Arrange two referees** early.
6. **Submit before the deadline.** Late applications are not accepted.
7. **Wait for the screening result.**
8. **If shortlisted,** prepare documents and attend the interview.
9. **Apply to universities** and secure an unconditional offer before the deadline.
10. **Receive final results,** then prepare for visa and travel.

---

## 🏫 Choosing Your Three Courses

1. Choose courses that connect to your **career plan** in your home country.
2. Check entry requirements and **English language** needs for each university.
3. Make sure the course is **one year, full-time, and eligible** for Chevening.
4. Choose a variety of universities to improve your chances of an offer.
5. Explain in your essays why each course fits your goals.

---

## ✍️ Writing the Essays

Chevening typically asks four essays covering these areas (confirm the exact questions in the current application):

| Essay | What the panel wants to see |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| **Leadership and influencing** | A real situation where you led, persuaded, or created change |
| **Networking** | How you build relationships and how Chevening networks will help |
| **Studying in the UK** | Why these courses and universities, and what you will learn |
| **Career plan** | Clear short-term and long-term goals, and how the master's helps |

**Simple structure**
```
Situation  -> The context and the challenge
Action     -> What you personally did
Result     -> Measurable outcome or clear change
Link       -> Why this matters for your plan and for Chevening
```

---

## 🎤 Interview Preparation

- Interviews are held at the British Embassy or High Commission and scored against the Chevening criteria.
- Review your essays and be ready to give new examples.
- Expect questions about leadership, influencing, networking, your course choices, and your plan after the master's.
- Practice clear, short answers with specific examples.

## 💡 Tips

- **Start early.** Strong essays take several drafts.
- **Use specific examples** with numbers and outcomes.
- **Show leadership in any setting,** not only formal job titles.
- **Link your plan to your home country's needs.**
- **Choose referees who know you well** and brief them.
- **Apply to universities early** so you can meet the offer deadline.
- **Submit well before the closing time** to avoid technical problems.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Not meeting the work experience requirement
- Choosing courses that are not eligible (for example not one year)
- Generic essays with no real examples
- Weak or unrelated career plan
- Waiting until the last day to submit
- Missing the deadline for an unconditional offer

---

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- Chevening **never charges** an application fee.
- Apply only through the official Chevening website.
- Be careful with agents who promise guaranteed selection for money.
