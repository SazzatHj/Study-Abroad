## 🌴 Overview

Malaysia offers government-funded postgraduate scholarships through two different ministries:

| Scholarship | Ministry | Level |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| **MIS** (Malaysia International Scholarship) | Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE) | Master's and PhD |
| **MTCP** (Malaysian Technical Cooperation Programme) | Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Wisma Putra) | Master's (check the current call for other levels) |

You may apply to both MIS and MTCP, since they are separate programs under different ministries (confirmed in the MTCP FAQ).

## ⚖️ MIS vs MTCP

| | **MIS** | **MTCP** |
|---|---|---|
| **Run by** | Ministry of Higher Education | Ministry of Foreign Affairs |
| **Target** | Outstanding international graduates from eligible countries | Applicants from MTCP-recognised developing countries |
| **Levels** | Master's, PhD | Mainly Master's |
| **Application portal** | MOHE MIS online system | MTCP scholarship portal |
| **Tuition** | Covered | Covered |
| **Monthly allowance** | Reported around RM 1,500 | Reported around RM 3,500 (verify) |
| **Fields** | Listed priority fields, mostly in selected universities | Fields stated in each year's call |
| **Can apply to both?** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |

> Allowance amounts are reported by secondary sources and may change. Check the official guidelines.

## 1. 🎓 Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS)

**Provider:** Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE), Malaysia

**Purpose:** Attract outstanding international talent for full-time postgraduate study in Malaysia.

**Levels:** Master's and PhD (full-time)

**Who can apply (common conditions):**
- Graduates from the recipient countries listed for that year
- Applying for full-time study at an eligible Malaysian university
- Meeting the academic, age, and English requirements in the guidelines
- Eligible study fields as listed in the guidelines (for example engineering, agriculture and veterinary, health and welfare excluding medicine, nursing and pharmacy, and other priority areas)

**Reported benefits:**
- Tuition fee coverage
- Monthly living allowance (reported around RM 1,500)
- Airfare support (reported; confirm in the guidelines)
- Other benefits as stated in that year's guidelines

**How it works:**
- Apply through the **official MOHE MIS online portal**
- Research Master's/PhD applicants usually submit a **research proposal** (the guidelines have specified a minimum length)
- No application fee

**Official links:**
- 🔗 MIS portal: https://biasiswa.mohe.gov.my/INTER/index.php
- 🔗 MIS guidelines: available on the MIS portal (look for the "Guidelines MIS" PDF of the current year)

## 2. 🤝 MTCP Scholarship

**Provider:** Ministry of Foreign Affairs Malaysia (under the Malaysian Technical Cooperation Programme)

**Purpose:** Support development cooperation by offering postgraduate study to citizens of developing countries.

**Level:** Mainly Master's (typically about 24–36 months, as reported)

**Who can apply (common conditions):**
- Citizens of countries recognised under the MTCP list
- Meeting the academic and English requirements in the call
- Applying to participating Malaysian universities and listed programs

**Reported benefits:**
- Tuition fee coverage
- Monthly allowance (reported around RM 3,500; verify)
- Additional support such as airfare and accommodation assistance, as stated in the call

**How it works:**
- Apply through the **MTCP online scholarship portal** during the annual call
- The call has recently opened around **May to June**

**Official links:**
- 🔗 MTCP portal: https://mtcp.kln.gov.my
- 🔗 MTCP FAQ and guidelines: published on the MTCP portal each year

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application

1. **Check the current guidelines** on the MIS or MTCP portal.
2. **Check country eligibility** for your nationality.
3. **Confirm the level and field** you want (Master's or PhD).
4. **Shortlist universities** and programs that are eligible.
5. **Write your research proposal or study plan** (clear problem, method, expected outcome).
6. **Book and take the English test** if required.
7. **Gather documents:** transcripts, certificates, references, passport, medical report if requested.
8. **Create an account and submit** through the official portal before the deadline.
9. **Contact universities** about admission as the process requires.
10. **Wait for the results,** then complete visa and pre-departure steps.

## 🏫 Choosing a University and Supervisor

1. Read the list of **eligible universities** in the current guidelines.
2. Check that your **field and level** are supported.
3. Search for **supervisors** whose research matches your plan.
4. Send a short, polite email with your CV and research summary.
5. Keep a record of replies in your tracker.

**Sample email to a supervisor**
```
Subject: Prospective postgraduate student - [Your Name], [Field]

Dear Professor [Last Name],

I am [Your Name], a [degree] graduate in [field] from [University],
[Country]. I read your work on [topic] and I am interested in
pursuing [Master's/PhD] research under your supervision.

I plan to apply for the Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) /
MTCP Scholarship. May I send you my CV and a short research proposal
for your feedback?

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
[Name]
[Email]
```

## 💡 Tips

- **Apply to both** MIS and MTCP when you are eligible. It improves your chances.
- **Prepare the research proposal early.** It is often the most important part for research programs.
- **Take the English test early** so you can retake it if needed.
- **Choose universities and fields listed in the guidelines.** Otherwise your application can be rejected.
- **Contact supervisors** before applying. A clear match strengthens your case.
- **Use the official portal only,** and submit a few days before the deadline.
- **Keep copies** of all submitted documents and confirmation emails.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Missing the short application window (MIS ran only about one month in 2026)
- Applying from a country that is not on the eligible list
- Choosing an ineligible university or field
- Weak or too-short research proposal
- Submitting expired or incomplete documents
- Using unofficial websites or agents for the application

---

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- MIS and MTCP **do not charge** an application fee.
- Do not pay agents for "guaranteed" scholarships.
- Apply only through the official government portals listed below.
- If you receive a suspicious email, verify it with the Ministry or the Malaysian Embassy/High Commission.
