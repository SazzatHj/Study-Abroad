# 🇨🇳 Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC / CGS)

> A practical guide to the Chinese Government Scholarship administered by the China Scholarship Council (CSC): types, eligibility, benefits, timeline, and how to apply through a university (Type B) or an embassy (Type A).
> **Last updated:** November 2026

> ⚠️ **Important:** The CSC scholarship is a group of programmes, not one competition with a single deadline. Rules, quotas, and dates differ by route, university, and country. Always confirm details on the official portal and the university's admissions page.

## 🌟 Overview

The **Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS)** is funded by China's Ministry of Education and administered by the **China Scholarship Council (CSC)**. It supports international students to study in China at designated universities.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Provider** | Government of China (Ministry of Education) |
| **Administrator** | China Scholarship Council (CSC) |
| **Levels** | Bachelor's, Master's, PhD (and some non-degree programmes) |
| **Language** | Chinese-taught or English-taught programmes, depending on the university |
| **Application** | Online through the CSC system, plus the university's own portal for Type B |
| **Open to** | International students (not Chinese citizens) |

---

## 🛤️ Application Routes: Type A, B, and C

| Route | Where you apply | Notes |
|-------|-----------------|-------|
| **Type A (Bilateral programmes)** | Through the Chinese Embassy or the government body in your home country | Competitive, with country quotas. Often has fuller benefits |
| **Type B (Chinese University Programme)** | Directly to a CSC-designated Chinese university | Most common route for individuals. You select Type B in the CSC system and enter the university's agency number |
| **Type C (Partner organisations)** | Through specific international organisations and programmes | Limited routes. Check the official list |

> For Type B, many universities require you to submit in **both** the CSC system and the university's own application portal. Missing either one can stop your nomination.

---

## ✅ Who Can Apply

Common conditions. The official guidelines for the year are the final authority.

- **Nationality:** Non-Chinese citizen in good health.
- **Education:**
  - Bachelor's: high school graduate
  - Master's: bachelor's degree
  - PhD: master's degree
- **Age limits (commonly stated):** Master's applicants under about 35 and PhD applicants under about 40. Check the current rule.
- **Language:**
  - Chinese-taught programmes usually need an HSK result.
  - English-taught programmes usually need an English proficiency result such as IELTS or TOEFL, or proof of English-medium study.
- **Admission:** Meet the university's own admission requirements. CSC selection is separate from university admission.
- **Other:** A study plan or research proposal, recommendation letters, and a physical examination record are usually required.

---

## 💰 Benefits

Benefits depend on the route, level, and university. Confirm in the official guidelines.

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Tuition** | Waived |
| **Accommodation** | Dormitory on campus, or an accommodation allowance |
| **Monthly living allowance** | Commonly provided under Type A and many programmes. Reported at about CNY 3,000 for master's and about CNY 3,500 for PhD (confirm). Some Type B arrangements differ by university |
| **Medical insurance** | Comprehensive insurance for the study period |
| **Other** | Possible one-time settlement allowance and other support, depending on the programme |

| **Dec – Mar** | Submit to the CSC system and the university portal |
| **Feb – Apr** | Many university deadlines (varies) |
| **Mar – Jun** | University review, nomination to CSC |
| **May – Jul** | CSC final decisions and results announced |
| **Jul – Aug** | Admission notice and visa documents |
| **Sep** | Arrival in China and start of study |

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application (Type B)

1. **Choose your level and field.**
2. **Shortlist universities** that offer CSC Type B and your programme (check the language of instruction).
3. **Read the university's CSC announcement** for requirements, agency number, and deadline.
4. **Prepare your study plan or research proposal.**
5. **Contact potential supervisors** (especially for research master's and PhD).
6. **Register on the CSC online system** and complete the application: choose **Type B** and enter the university's **agency number**.
7. **Apply on the university's own international student portal** and upload the same core documents.
8. **Pay any university application fee** if required.
9. **Submit before the university's deadline.**
10. **Wait for the university's nomination,** then CSC's final decision.

## 📂 Documents Checklist

Check the university's list. Common documents:

- [ ] CSC online application form
- [ ] University online application
- [ ] Passport copy (valid)
- [ ] Highest degree certificate and transcripts (notarised if required)
- [ ] Study plan or research proposal
- [ ] Two recommendation letters (professors or associate professors)
- [ ] Language proof (HSK, or IELTS/TOEFL or English-medium proof)
- [ ] Physical examination form
- [ ] Police clearance or no-criminal-record certificate (if required)
- [ ] CV and publications (for research programmes)
- [ ] Acceptance letter from a supervisor (if requested)

## 🏫 Choosing a University and Supervisor

1. Check that the university is **CSC-designated** and offers **Type B** for your level.
2. Confirm the programme is taught in a language you can handle.
3. For research degrees, **email potential supervisors** with your CV and research plan.
4. Compare university location, ranking, field strength, and support for international students.
5. Apply to more than one university where the rules allow.

**Sample email to a supervisor**
```
Subject: Prospective CSC applicant - [Your Name], [Field]

Dear Professor [Last Name],

I am [Your Name], a [degree] graduate in [field] from [University],
[Country]. I read your work on [topic] and I am very interested in
pursuing [Master's/PhD] research under your supervision.

I plan to apply for the Chinese Government Scholarship (Type B) at
[University]. May I send you my CV and research proposal for your
feedback?

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
[Name]
[Email]
```

## 💡 Tips

- **Apply early.** Some universities select on a rolling basis.
- **Submit to both systems** (CSC and the university) for Type B.
- **Tailor your study plan** to each university and supervisor.
- **Contact supervisors** before applying for research degrees.
- **Check notarisation and translation rules** for your documents.
- **Book language tests** early (HSK or IELTS/TOEFL).
- **Keep records** of agency numbers, deadlines, and portal logins.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Submitting only on the CSC system and forgetting the university portal (or the reverse)
- Entering the wrong agency number or program type
- Missing the university's own deadline
- Using a generic study plan
- Applying to programmes that are not CSC-eligible
- Trusting unofficial agents instead of university and CSC websites

---

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- The CSC scholarship **does not require** payment for selection.
- Be careful with agents offering guaranteed CSC placements for a fee.
- Some universities charge a small **application fee** for their own portal. That is separate from the scholarship, so confirm it on the university's official site.
- Use only official CSC, Chinese Embassy, and university websites.
