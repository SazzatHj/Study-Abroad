## 🌟 Overview

The **Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)** is a fully funded scholarship of the Government of the Republic of Korea. It is run by the **National Institute for International Education (NIIED)** under the Ministry of Education.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Provider** | Government of South Korea (Ministry of Education) |
| **Administrator** | NIIED |
| **Formerly known as** | KGSP (Korean Government Scholarship Program) |
| **Levels** | Undergraduate (GKS-U), Graduate: Master's and PhD (GKS-G) |
| **Funding** | Fully funded (tuition, allowance, airfare, language training, insurance) |
| **Language** | Korean or English-taught programs, depending on the university |
| **Eligible applicants** | Citizens of countries designated by NIIED |

---

## 🎓 GKS Programs

| Program | Level | Duration | Notes |
|---------|-------|----------|-------|
| **GKS-U (Undergraduate)** | Bachelor's | About 5 years (1 year Korean language + 4 years degree; longer for some majors) | Applicants must not already hold a bachelor's degree |
| **GKS-G (Graduate)** | Master's / PhD | Language year + degree (about 3 years Master's total, up to about 4–5 years PhD) | Master's and PhD applicants have different eligibility rules |
| **GKS Associate Program** | Associate degree | Varies | University track only (limited seats) |
| **UIC Program** | Bachelor's | Varies | University track, selected institutions |

> Each year's guidelines list the exact programs, slots, and participating universities.

---

## 🛤️ Embassy Track vs University Track

| | **Embassy Track** | **University Track** |
|---|---|---|
| **Where you apply** | Korean Embassy/Consulate in your country (via the Study in Korea portal for undergraduate) | Directly to a designated Korean university |
| **Quota** | Slots per country | Slots per university |
| **Selection** | Embassy screening, then NIIED final selection | University selection, then NIIED final approval |
| **Who it suits** | Applicants who want to choose multiple universities and apply through their home country | Applicants who target a specific university program |
| **Note** | Applicants usually rank several universities | Follow that university's own schedule and documents |

> You can apply to one track per cycle unless the guidelines say otherwise. Read the rule for the current year.

## 💰 Benefits

Amounts are approximate and updated by NIIED. Confirm in the current guidelines.

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Airfare** | Round-trip economy ticket |
| **Settlement allowance** | One-time payment on arrival |
| **Monthly allowance** | Roughly KRW 900,000–1,000,000 per month (differs by level) |
| **Tuition** | Full tuition and admission fees covered by NIIED |
| **Korean language training** | 1 year of Korean language training at a designated institute |
| **Research allowance** | For graduate students (yearly) |
| **Medical insurance** | Monthly stipend for health insurance |
| **Language proficiency grant** | Extra support for strong TOPIK results (for example TOPIK level 5–6) |
| **Dissertation printing** | For graduate students |

## ✅ General Eligibility

Conditions below are common. The annual guidelines are the final authority.

- **Nationality:** Citizen of a country on NIIED's list. Applicants and **both parents** must be citizens of that country, and not Korean citizens.
- **Age:**
  - Undergraduate: generally **under 25** (born on or after a cutoff date in the guidelines)
  - Graduate: generally **under 40**
- **Education:**
  - Undergraduate: high school graduate (or expected) with no bachelor's degree
  - Master's: bachelor's degree (or expected)
  - PhD: master's degree (or expected)
- **Grades:** Strong academic record. Commonly a grade average of about **80%** or equivalent (or top of the class), as defined in the guidelines.
- **Health:** Good physical and mental health to study in Korea.
- **Previous Korean degrees/scholarships:** Restrictions may apply to those who studied in Korea before.
- **Language:** Not always required to apply, but TOPIK or English proficiency can help and is required by some universities.

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application

1. **Check the official guidelines** on Study in Korea (for undergraduate) or the Embassy site (for graduate).
2. **Check country eligibility and quota** for your nationality.
3. **Pick your track:** Embassy or University.
4. **Choose universities and majors** (Embassy Track usually requires a ranked list).
5. **Prepare documents:** transcripts, certificates, recommendation letters, statement, study plan, health certificate, and language proof.
6. **Get documents apostilled/authenticated** if the guidelines require it.
7. **Submit your application** by the deadline (online via Study in Korea for undergraduate Embassy Track, or as instructed by the Embassy/University).
8. **Attend the interview** if shortlisted.
9. **Wait for NIIED's final selection.**
10. **Prepare to depart:** visa, flight, and arrival procedures. Attend the Korean language year as required.

## 📂 Documents Checklist

Exact list depends on the level and track. Plan for these:

- [ ] Application form (official GKS form)
- [ ] Personal statement
- [ ] Study plan (and research plan for graduate)
- [ ] Letter(s) of recommendation (school principal or professor)
- [ ] Academic transcripts (all schools/universities)
- [ ] Certificate of graduation (or expected graduation)
- [ ] Copy of passport (and parents' nationality proof if requested)
- [ ] Health certificate or medical form
- [ ] Language proof (TOPIK, English test, or institution certificate if available)
- [ ] Proof of citizenship (both parents)
- [ ] Awards and other certificates (optional, limited number)
- [ ] Apostille or authentication (when required)

## 📝 Selection Process

| Stage | What happens |
|-------|--------------|
| **1. Document screening** | The Embassy or university checks eligibility and quality of documents |
| **2. Interview** | Questions about your goals, field, and why Korea |
| **3. Recommendation to NIIED** | Selected candidates are recommended |
| **4. Final selection** | NIIED announces the final GKS recipients |

**Interview preparation**
- Be ready to explain: *Why Korea? Why this major? What will you do after graduation?*
- Learn basic facts about Korea and your chosen university.
- Practice explaining your study plan clearly in a few minutes.

## 🏫 Choosing Universities and Majors

1. Read the list of **designated universities** for your track in the current guidelines.
2. Check whether each university offers your major in **English or Korean**.
3. Compare location, ranking, and language requirements.
4. For graduate study, **contact professors** to discuss research fit.
5. Rank your choices carefully (Embassy Track).

**Sample email to a professor**
```
Subject: Inquiry about GKS-G research opportunity - [Your Name], [Field]

Dear Professor [Last Name],

I am [Your Name], a [degree] graduate in [field] from [University],
[Country]. I read your paper "[Title]" and I am very interested in
your work on [topic].

I plan to apply for the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS-G) for graduate
study. Could you let me know if you are accepting graduate students in
your lab? I would be happy to share my CV and research plan.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
[Name]
[Email]
```

## 💡 Tips for a Strong Application

- **Start early.** Authentication and recommendation letters take time.
- **Follow the guidelines exactly.** Wrong format, missing signatures, or missing pages can lead to rejection.
- **Write a clear, honest statement.** Explain your goals and how Korea fits them.
- **Show your academic strength:** grades, projects, awards.
- **Learn Korean.** Even basic TOPIK helps your application and your life in Korea.
- **Make your study plan specific.** Courses, skills, and career goals.
- **Pick a realistic university list** that matches your profile.
- **Keep copies** of every submitted document.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Missing the Embassy or university deadline
- Using last year's forms or guidelines
- Applying to both tracks when it is not allowed
- Skipping authentication (apostille) when it is required
- Weak or copied personal statement
- Not checking age and grade requirements
- Relying on unofficial blogs instead of official announcements

---

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- GKS **does not charge** any application or selection fee.
- Do not trust agents who promise a "guaranteed GKS" for money.
- Use only official sources: `studyinkorea.go.kr`, `niied.go.kr`, and the Korean Embassy website.
- If in doubt, contact the Korean Embassy directly.
