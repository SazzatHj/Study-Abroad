## 🗓️ Planning Calendar

A rough yearly view to help you plan. Exact dates vary by country and year.

| Month | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| **Jan** | Türkiye Bursları window opens (expected). Erasmus Mundus deadlines. Prepare for Australia Awards and GKS-G |
| **Feb** | Australia Awards opens (1 Feb). Türkiye Bursları closes (about 20 Feb). Many CSC deadlines. GKS-G window may open |
| **Mar** | MIS opens. MEXT recruitment announcements begin |
| **Apr** | Australia Awards closes (30 Apr). MEXT Embassy applications |
| **May** | MTCP call may open. MEXT screening begins |
| **Jun – Aug** | Interviews and exams for several programs. Fulbright and DAAD course deadlines begin. Chevening opens in early August. Prepare for UN–Nippon |
| **Sep** | UN–Nippon deadline. GKS-U guidelines released |
| **Oct – Nov** | Chevening closes (early Oct). Erasmus Mundus and CSC Type B open. GKS-U University Track windows |
| **Dec** | Review results, improve English scores, gather documents |

## 🧭 How to Use This Folder

1. **Pick your level** (Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD) and check which guides match it.
2. **Open the guide** and read the *Eligibility* and *Current Cycle Status* sections first.
3. **Check the official links** inside the guide and confirm the latest rules.
4. **Copy the Application Tracker** from each guide into your own notes and update it as you go.
5. **Use the checklists** to prepare documents early.
6. **Apply to more than one** scholarship when the rules allow it. It improves your chances.

## ✅ Universal Application Checklist

Most scholarships ask for similar things. Prepare these early:

- [ ] Valid passport (check expiry date)
- [ ] Academic transcripts and degree certificates
- [ ] English test score (IELTS, TOEFL, PTE) or other required language proof
- [ ] Updated CV or resume
- [ ] Statement of purpose or motivation letter
- [ ] Study plan or research proposal
- [ ] 2–3 recommendation letters
- [ ] Medical or health certificate (when required)
- [ ] Work experience letters (when required)
- [ ] Certificates, awards, and publications
- [ ] Certified or apostilled copies (when required)
- [ ] Passport-size photographs

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- Genuine government scholarships **do not charge** application or selection fees.
- Be careful with agents, Telegram groups, or WhatsApp offers that promise guaranteed selection.
- Use only official government and embassy websites for applications.
