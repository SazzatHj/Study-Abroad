## 🌟 Overview

**Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM)** are integrated international master's programmes delivered jointly by universities in several countries, funded by the **European Union** through Erasmus+. Selected students receive a **scholarship** covering the whole programme.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Provider** | European Union (Erasmus+), managed by EACEA |
| **Level** | Master's (typically 12 to 24 months) |
| **Structure** | Study at two or more universities in different countries |
| **Degree** | Joint, double, or multiple degree, depending on the programme |
| **Open to** | Students worldwide, including those from partner countries |
| **Application** | Directly to each chosen programme |

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

- You choose a **Joint Master's programme** from the official Erasmus Mundus catalogue.
- You study in **different countries** according to the programme's mobility plan.
- You apply for the **scholarship through the programme** (usually in the same application).
- Scholarship quotas are limited, and many programmes have separate quotas for partner-country students.

---

## ✅ Who Can Apply

Common conditions. Each programme's page is the final authority.

- **Education:** A bachelor's degree (or equivalent) that meets the programme's entry requirements, or be in the final year of it.
- **Language:** English (or the programme's language) proficiency, often with a test such as IELTS or TOEFL.
- **Maximum applications:** You may apply to **up to three** Joint Master's programmes in one intake.
- **Previous scholarship:** If you already received an EMJM scholarship before, you are generally **not eligible** for another.
- **Age limit:** There is generally no age limit, but follow each programme's rules.
- **Nationality and residence:** Open to many nationalities. Some rules differ for students from partner countries and programme countries.

---

## 💰 Benefits

Amounts depend on the year and the student's category. Confirm in the programme information.

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Tuition and participation costs** | Covered by the scholarship |
| **Monthly living allowance** | Paid for the duration of the programme |
| **Travel and installation costs** | Contribution for students who qualify |
| **Insurance** | Included as stated by the programme |
| **Mobility** | Study periods and experiences across several countries |

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application

1. **Open the official Erasmus Mundus catalogue** and search by field.
2. **Shortlist programmes** that fit your degree and goals.
3. **Visit each programme website** to read entry requirements, quotas, and deadlines.
4. **Pick up to three programmes** and plan your calendar.
5. **Prepare documents** (see checklist below).
6. **Apply through each programme's online portal** and include the scholarship request.
7. **Submit before each deadline.**
8. **Wait for decisions,** then confirm your acceptance and prepare for visa and travel.

---

## 📂 Documents Checklist

Requirements differ by programme.

- [ ] Application form (programme portal)
- [ ] Motivation letter
- [ ] CV (often Europass or a set format)
- [ ] Bachelor's degree certificate and transcripts
- [ ] English language test result (if required)
- [ ] Recommendation letters (often two)
- [ ] Passport copy
- [ ] Proof of relevant experience (if requested)
- [ ] Research or study interest statement (if requested)
- [ ] Any programme-specific documents

---

## 🎓 Choosing Programmes

1. Pick programmes in your **field and interest**, not only the most famous ones.
2. Check the **countries and universities** where you will study.
3. Compare **language and entry requirements**.
4. Check **scholarship quotas** (especially for partner-country students).
5. Choose three programmes with different levels of competition, so you have realistic options.

---

## ✍️ Motivation Letter

A strong motivation letter is usually the most important part.

**Suggested structure**
```
1. Introduction   -> Who you are and the programme you apply to
2. Background     -> Your education, work, projects relevant to the programme
3. Why this master's -> What in the curriculum and mobility attracts you
4. Goals          -> Career plan and how the programme helps
5. Contribution   -> What you will bring to the cohort and your home community
```

Write a **separate, tailored letter** for each programme.

## 💡 Tips

- **Start early,** because each programme has separate requirements.
- **Use all three applications** if you are eligible.
- **Tailor each motivation letter** to the specific programme.
- **Book your English test early.**
- **Ask referees in advance** and give them your CV and goals.
- **Read the programme's selection criteria** and address them directly.
- **Keep a calendar** of all deadlines and follow-up steps.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Looking for a central Erasmus Mundus portal and missing programme deadlines
- Using the same motivation letter for all programmes
- Applying to more than three programmes
- Not reading scholarship quota rules for your category
- Submitting incomplete or late documents
- Ignoring language requirements

---

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- Erasmus Mundus programmes **do not sell** scholarships or guaranteed places.
- Be careful with agents who promise guaranteed Erasmus Mundus selection for money.
- Use only the official Erasmus+ and programme websites.
