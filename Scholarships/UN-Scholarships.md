## 1. 🌊 UN–Nippon Foundation Fellowship

**Offered by:** UN Division for Ocean Affairs and the Law of the Sea (DOALOS) & The Nippon Foundation (Japan)

**Focus:** Ocean affairs, law of the sea, marine policy, ocean governance, sustainable use of the oceans

**Duration:** About 9 months, in two phases:
- **Phase 1:** Training at UN Headquarters, New York
- **Phase 2:** Research at a partner host institution

**Who can apply:**
- Mid-level professionals from developing States working in ocean-related fields (government, research, policy)
- Generally between **25–45 years old** (check the current call for the exact age limit)
- At least a first university degree or equivalent
- Nomination/recommendation from the applicant's employer or institution is part of the application

**Benefits:** Monthly stipend, travel, insurance and other support (see the official call for details)

**Deadline pattern:** The call for the next session is usually announced around mid-year, with a deadline in **September**. (The call for the 2027 session had a deadline of 13 September 2026, so the next call is expected in 2027.)

**Official links:**
- 🔗 Fellowship site: https://www.un.org/oceancapacity/UNNF
- 🔗 Apply page: https://www.un.org/oceancapacity/content/apply-now
- 📧 Contact: doalos@un.org

> 📝 ভিডিওতে দেওয়া পুরোনো লিংক (`un.org/depts/los/nippon`) এখন সরে গেছে। উপরের নতুন লিংক ব্যবহার করুন।

## 2. 🕊️ UNODA Disarmament Fellowship

**Offered by:** UN Office for Disarmament Affairs (UNODA)

**Focus:** Disarmament, arms control, non-proliferation, international security and peace

**Who can apply:**
- Typically **government officials** (diplomats, ministry staff) nominated by their member state
- Not usually a standard open application for individuals

**What to do if you are a student/graduate:**
- Check whether your government's foreign ministry nominates candidates
- Look at UNODA's youth and education programmes as an alternative

**Official links:**
- 🔗 UNODA: https://disarmament.unoda.org
- 🔗 UN Disarmament: https://www.un.org/disarmament

## 3. 🎓 UNHCR DAFI Scholarship

**Offered by:** UNHCR (UN Refugee Agency), funded mainly by the German government

**Full name:** Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI)

**Focus:** Undergraduate higher education for refugees in their first country of asylum

**Who can apply:**
- ✅ Recognised refugees (and in some cases returnees) living in a host country where DAFI operates
- ❌ **Not** for general international students

**How to apply:**
- Applications are handled **locally** through the UNHCR office or partner organisation in the host country
- Contact the UNHCR country office in the country where you live

**Official links:**
- 🔗 UNHCR Education: https://www.unhcr.org/education
- 🔗 DAFI information: search "UNHCR DAFI" on the UNHCR website

## 4. 🌐 UN University (UNU) Scholarships

**Offered by:** United Nations University, with institutes in several countries

**Focus:** Master's and PhD programmes in sustainability, development, environment, social sciences, and related fields

**Important:** Each UNU institute has **its own programmes, scholarships and deadlines**. Check the specific institute and programme page.

**How to apply:**
1. Choose the programme and institute
2. Read the admission requirements and scholarship section on that programme page
3. Apply for admission, and apply for the scholarship in the same application if the programme allows it

**Official links:**
- 🔗 UNU Admissions: https://www.unu.edu/admissions
- 🔗 UNU home: https://unu.edu

## ✅ Application Checklist

- [ ] Eligibility সম্পূর্ণ যাচাই করা হয়েছে
- [ ] অফিসিয়াল ওয়েবসাইটে সর্বশেষ call ও deadline দেখা হয়েছে

- [ ] Academic transcripts ও certificates স্ক্যান করা হয়েছে
- [ ] Passport কপি
- [ ] Statement of Purpose / Motivation letter লেখা হয়েছে
- [ ] Recommendation letters সংগ্রহ করা হয়েছে
- [ ] English proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL, যদি লাগে)

- [ ] Submission confirmation email সংরক্ষণ করা হয়েছে

## 💡 Tips & Scam Alert

**Tips**
- অফিসিয়াল সাইট (`un.org`, `unhcr.org`, `unu.edu`) থেকে তথ্য নিন; ব্লগ ও ভিডিওর তথ্য পুরোনো হতে পারে।
- Deadline-এর অন্তত ২-৩ সপ্তাহ আগে আবেদন গুছিয়ে ফেলুন।
- Recommendation letter-এর জন্য রেফারিদের আগেভাগে অনুরোধ করুন।
- নিজের কাজের সঙ্গে প্রোগ্রামের লক্ষ্য কীভাবে মেলে, তা Statement-এ পরিষ্কার লিখুন।

**Scam Alert 🚨**
- জাতিসংঘ কখনো আবেদন ফি বা "selection fee" চায় না।
- Telegram/WhatsApp-এর মাধ্যমে "নিশ্চিত স্কলারশিপ" দেওয়ার প্রস্তাব এলে সাবধান থাকুন।
- সন্দেহ হলে সরাসরি অফিসিয়াল ইমেইলে যোগাযোগ করুন।
