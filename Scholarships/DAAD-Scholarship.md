## 🌟 Overview

The **DAAD (Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst, German Academic Exchange Service)** is Germany's main organisation for international academic exchange. It offers scholarships for master's, doctoral, and research study in Germany.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Provider** | DAAD, funded mainly by the German government |
| **Levels** | Master's, PhD, research stays, and some other programmes |
| **Language** | German or English, depending on the course |
| **Key point** | There is **no single DAAD application**. Each programme has its own process |

---

## 🧭 Main DAAD Programmes

| Programme | Level | Who it is for | How to apply |
|-----------|-------|---------------|--------------|
| **EPOS** (Development-Related Postgraduate Courses) | Master's (doctoral only in exceptional cases) | Graduates from developing and newly industrialised countries with work experience | **Directly to the course** (university) |
| **Helmut Schmidt Programme** | Master's in public policy and good governance | Professionals from developing countries | Through the DAAD portal or programme process (check the call) |
| **Study Scholarships for Graduates** (for example arts, music, architecture) | Master's | Graduates in specific fields | DAAD portal, deadlines by field |
| **Research Grants** | Doctoral and research | Doctoral students and young researchers | DAAD portal, deadlines by programme |

> This guide focuses on EPOS. Check the DAAD scholarship database for other programmes that match your profile.

## 🎓 EPOS: Development-Related Postgraduate Courses

EPOS gives **individual scholarships** for selected postgraduate courses in Germany that are listed by DAAD. The courses are linked to development-related fields such as economics, public policy, public health, environment, education, engineering, and more.

**Key rules:**
- You apply **directly to the university course**, not through one DAAD portal.
- Each course has its **own deadline**, often almost **one year before** the start (for example, an October 2026 deadline for study starting October 2027).
- Only courses on DAAD's official list for that intake can offer EPOS scholarships.
- Some courses run only every two years.

---

## ✅ Who Can Apply (EPOS)

Common conditions. The official EPOS page and the course website are the final authority.

- **Country:** Citizen of a developing or newly industrialised country on DAAD's eligible list.
- **Degree:** A university degree (usually a bachelor's) that meets the course's admission requirements.
- **Work experience:** Typically **at least two years of relevant professional experience** after your degree.
- **Recent degree:** DAAD often limits how long ago your degree was completed (commonly within a few years). Check the current rule.
- **Language:** English and/or German proficiency, depending on the course.
- **Return plan:** Intention to return to your home country and contribute to development there.
- **Not already in Germany for long:** There are rules about previous stays in Germany. Check the current guidelines.

## 💰 Benefits

Amounts change. Confirm in the current DAAD information.

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Monthly stipend** | Around €1,000 per month for master's students (reported around €992; confirm) |
| **Tuition and fees** | Study fees as covered by the scholarship terms |
| **Health, accident, and liability insurance** | Included |
| **Travel allowance** | Lump sum for travel to and from Germany |
| **Study and research allowance** | Annual contribution |
| **German language course** | Pre-course language support where applicable |
| **Extras** | Possible rent subsidy, allowances for family members, and other benefits |

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application

1. **Open the DAAD EPOS page** and read the programme rules.
2. **View the list of EPOS courses and deadlines** for your intake.
3. **Shortlist courses** that fit your degree, experience, and career goals.
4. **Read each course's website** for admission requirements and documents.
5. **Prepare documents** (see checklist below).
6. **Apply directly to the university course** and include the DAAD scholarship application as instructed.
7. **Submit before the course deadline.**
8. **Wait for selection.** The university and DAAD decide separately.
9. **If selected,** prepare visa, insurance, and travel.

## 📂 Documents Checklist

Exact documents vary by course. Common ones:

- [ ] Application form (course and/or DAAD form)
- [ ] Motivation letter
- [ ] CV (often in Europass or a set format)
- [ ] Degree certificate and transcripts
- [ ] Proof of work experience (letters from employers)
- [ ] Language certificate (English and/or German)
- [ ] Letters of recommendation
- [ ] Passport copy
- [ ] APS certificate (required for some countries)
- [ ] Certified or translated copies as required

---

## 🏫 Choosing a Course

1. Make sure the course is on **DAAD's EPOS list** for your intake year.
2. Match the course with your **degree and work experience**.
3. Check the **language of instruction** and language requirements.
4. Compare the **deadline** and required documents.
5. Apply to more than one eligible course if the rules allow, so you have backup options.

## 💡 Tips

- **Plan one year ahead.** EPOS deadlines are far before the start date.
- **Show relevant work experience** and how it connects to development goals.
- **Write a focused motivation letter** for each course.
- **Check language requirements early** and book the test in time.
- **Follow each course's document rules exactly,** since formats differ.
- **Keep notes** for every course's deadline.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Looking for a single DAAD portal and missing the course-specific application
- Applying to a course that is not on the EPOS list
- Missing the deadline, which is much earlier than the start of studies
- Not meeting the work experience requirement
- Submitting incomplete or uncertified documents
- Using a generic motivation letter

---

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- DAAD scholarships **do not require** application fees to DAAD.
- Be careful with agents promising guaranteed DAAD scholarships.
- Use only `daad.de` and the official course websites.
