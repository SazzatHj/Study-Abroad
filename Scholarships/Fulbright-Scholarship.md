## 🌟 Overview

The **Fulbright Foreign Student Program** is funded by the U.S. Government and brings students from about 160 countries to study at U.S. universities. It is run in each country by the **U.S. Embassy** or a **binational Fulbright Commission**.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Provider** | U.S. Department of State (Bureau of Educational and Cultural Affairs) |
| **Levels** | Mainly master's and PhD (varies by country) |
| **In Bangladesh** | Managed through the U.S. Embassy in Dhaka; the programme has offered master's study in the U.S. |
| **Selection focus** | Academic strength, leadership, community engagement, and commitment to your home country |
| **Visa** | J-1 exchange visitor visa, with a home-country return expectation |

---

## ✅ Who Can Apply

These are common conditions for Bangladesh. The official call for the year is the final authority.

- **Citizenship and residence:** Bangladeshi citizen living in Bangladesh throughout the application and selection process.
- **No dual US status:** Not a dual Bangladeshi-US citizen and not a US permanent resident.
- **US degree:** Not already holding a degree from a US university and not currently enrolled in a US degree program.
- **Education:** A bachelor's degree (and strong academic record) that meets the requirements stated in the call.
- **Experience:** Professional or community experience is often valued. Check whether the call requires a set amount.
- **English:** Strong English skills. Test requirements (such as TOEFL or IELTS) depend on the call and universities.
- **Return:** Willingness to return to Bangladesh and contribute after the programme.

## 💰 Benefits

Benefits can vary by year and placement. Typical items include:

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Tuition and fees** | Covered under the grant |
| **Living stipend** | Monthly support for living costs |
| **Airfare** | Round-trip international travel |
| **Health insurance** | Included |
| **Allowances** | Settling-in and book or supplies allowances |
| **Support** | J-1 visa sponsorship and pre-departure orientation |

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application

1. **Follow the U.S. Embassy Dhaka announcements** and read the full eligibility notice.
2. **Check your eligibility** carefully.
3. **Plan your study field** and why you need US education.
4. **Prepare documents:** transcripts, essays, references, CV, and English test results if required.
5. **Submit the online application** before the deadline.
6. **Attend interviews** if shortlisted.
7. **Complete placement** (the programme places finalists at US universities, or you may suggest options as instructed).
8. **Complete visa and pre-departure steps** after selection.

## ✍️ Selection Criteria and Essays

Fulbright looks for **academic merit plus leadership and service**, not only grades.

- **Academic excellence:** Strong record and clear study goals
- **Leadership:** Examples of initiative and impact
- **Community engagement:** Service and contribution beyond work or study
- **Cultural exchange:** Willingness to share your culture and learn about the US
- **Return plan:** How you will use your learning in Bangladesh

**Simple structure for your essays**
```
Opening    -> A short, specific story or moment that shows who you are
Impact     -> What you did and what changed because of it
Goals      -> What you want to study and why in the US
Return     -> How you will contribute back at home
```

---

## 🎤 Interview Preparation

- Be ready to explain *why this field, why the US, and what you will do after returning*.
- Prepare specific stories about leadership and community work.
- Show curiosity about other cultures and willingness to engage with Americans.
- Practice speaking clearly and concisely.

## 💡 Tips

- **Start early,** especially with essays and references.
- **Tell a real story.** Specific examples beat general claims.
- **Show service,** not only academic success.
- **Explain your return plan** with concrete ideas.
- **Choose recommenders** who can speak about your leadership and character.
- **Take the English test early** if it is required.
- **Follow instructions exactly** on word limits and file formats.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Treating Fulbright as one global application
- Ignoring the residence and citizenship rules
- Generic essays without personal stories
- No clear plan for returning home
- Weak or late recommendation letters
- Missing the deadline, which closes at 11:59 p.m. Bangladesh time on the stated date

---

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- Fulbright **does not charge** an application fee.
- Be careful with agents or social media pages promising guaranteed Fulbright selection.
- Use only the U.S. Embassy Dhaka website and the official Fulbright pages.
