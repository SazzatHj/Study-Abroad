## 🌏 Overview

**Australia Awards Scholarships** are long-term development awards funded by the **Australian Government's Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT)**. They aim to build leadership and skills in partner countries so that graduates can contribute to development when they return home.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Provider** | Australian Government (DFAT) |
| **Purpose** | Develop emerging leaders and strengthen workforce capability in partner countries |
| **Levels** | Mostly postgraduate (Master's, sometimes PhD). Levels offered depend on the country program |
| **Where** | Participating Australian universities |
| **Application system** | **OASIS** (Online Australia Awards Scholarships Information System) |
| **South Asia program** | Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka (and Mongolia) under the *Australia Awards, South Asia & Mongolia* program |
| **Return requirement** | Scholars are expected to return home and contribute to their country after studying |

---

## ✅ Who Can Apply

These are common conditions. Your country page and the **Scholarship Policy Handbook** are the final authority.

- **Citizenship and residence:** Citizen of a participating country (for Bangladesh: Bangladeshi citizen living in Bangladesh when applying).
- **Not Australian or New Zealand:** Not an Australian citizen, New Zealand citizen, or permanent resident of Australia, and generally not a dual citizen of these.
- **Academic background:** Meets the entry requirements for your chosen course (usually a bachelor's degree for Master's study).
- **English language:** Meets the English requirement of the university and the scholarship (IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, or similar).
- **Experience:** Work experience can be required or strongly valued, depending on the country.
- **Previous awards:** Past recipients may be ineligible for a number of years (see the handbook).
- **Priority fields:** Each country has development priority areas that are favored.
- **Inclusion:** Women, people with disability, and people from marginalised groups are encouraged to apply.
- **Intake levels (Bangladesh):** For the 2027 intake, Bangladesh was reported to accept Master's by coursework or research applicants. Check the Bangladesh country page for the current levels.

## 💰 Benefits

Benefits may be adjusted. Confirm in the handbook.

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Tuition** | Full tuition fees |
| **Airfare** | Return economy airfare |
| **Establishment allowance** | One-time payment to settle in |
| **Living contribution** | Fortnightly contribution towards living expenses |
| **Health cover** | Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) |
| **Introductory Academic Program** | Pre-course academic and English support, where applicable |
| **Supplementary academic support** | Extra help for scholars who need it |
| **Fieldwork allowance** | For eligible research or fieldwork components |
| **Reintegration support** | Support for returning home and applying skills |

Typical cycle for applicants from South Asia:

| Period | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **1 Feb** | Applications open on OASIS |
| **30 Apr** | Applications close (check exact time for your country) |
| **May – Jul** | Eligibility check and shortlisting |
| **Jul – Sep** | Interviews and further assessment |
| **Sep – Nov** | Selection outcome and offers |

| **Jan – Feb (next year)** | Pre-departure briefing and travel |
| **Feb or Jul (next year)** | Study begins in Australia |

> Dates vary by year and country. Treat this as a planning guide.

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Application

1. **Open the official page** for your country (for Bangladesh: Australia Awards Bangladesh pages on the South Asia & Mongolia site).
2. **Read the Scholarship Policy Handbook** and the country profile (priority fields, levels, special rules).
3. **Check eligibility.**
4. **Choose your course** and confirm it is eligible and available at participating universities.
5. **Create an OASIS account** and start your application early.
6. **Write the application responses** (motivation, leadership, development contribution, return plan).
7. **Upload documents** in the required format.
8. **Submit before the closing time.** Late or incomplete applications are not considered.
9. **Attend the interview** if shortlisted.
10. **If selected,** apply for university admission through the Awards process, complete health checks, and get your visa.

## 📝 Selection Process

| Stage | What happens |
|-------|--------------|
| **1. Eligibility check** | Applications are checked against requirements |
| **2. Assessment** | Applications are scored (leadership, development impact, academic merit, fit with priorities) |
| **3. Shortlisting** | Top candidates are shortlisted |
| **4. Interview** | Panel interview about your goals, leadership, and plan to contribute |
| **5. Selection** | Final selection and offer |
| **6. Admission** | University admission arranged within the Awards process |

**Interview preparation**
- Be ready for: *Why this course? Why Australia? How will you use your skills when you return? Tell us about a time you led change.*
- Use specific examples from your work or community.

## 🎓 Choosing a Course and University

1. List your **priority fields** from the country page (for example health, education, agriculture, governance, climate, energy, or economic development, depending on the year).
2. Look at the **list of participating universities** and their eligible courses.
3. Check that your course is **full-time, on-campus** and fits the program rules.
4. Compare course content, location, and support for international students.
5. Make sure the course connects clearly to a **development problem** in your country.

---

## ✍️ Writing a Strong Application

Australia Awards looks for **leadership potential and development impact**, not only grades.

- **Be specific.** Mention real problems, numbers, and outcomes you have worked on.
- **Show leadership**, even outside formal titles (community, volunteering, team projects).
- **Link study to impact:** What skill will you gain? How will you apply it at home?
- **Show a return plan:** The program expects you to come back and contribute.
- **Use your own words.** All responses must be original work.
- **Answer each question directly.** Do not repeat the same story in every response.

**Simple structure for each response**
```
Situation  -> What was the problem or context?
Action     -> What did you personally do?
Result     -> What changed? (numbers or clear outcomes)
Link       -> How does this connect to your study and future plan?
```

## 💡 Tips

- **Start 3–4 months before** the opening date. English tests and references take time.
- **Read the handbook fully.** Most rejections are for eligibility or incomplete documents.
- **Get the English test done early** so you can retake it if needed.
- **Keep evidence** of your work and leadership (letters, reports, photos, certificates).
- **Ask referees early** and brief them about the scholarship.
- **Proofread** your responses and ask a mentor to review.
- **Submit a few days early** to avoid system problems near the deadline.
- **Consider Australia Awards Short Courses** as a different opportunity for professionals.

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Applying after the OASIS closing time
- Choosing a course that is not eligible
- Generic answers with no real examples
- Not explaining how the study will benefit your home country
- Missing or weak references
- Ignoring the country's priority fields
- Copying text from other applications (all responses must be original)

---

## 🚨 Scam Alert

- Australia Awards **never charges** an application fee.
- Apply **only through OASIS** and official Australia Awards pages.
- Be careful with agents promising guaranteed selection for money.
- Check official sources: `dfat.gov.au` and `australiaawardssouthasiamongolia.org`.
