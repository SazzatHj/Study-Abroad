## 📖 How to Read This List

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Chihō (地方)** | One of Japan's 8 large geographic regions |
| **Prefecture (県/都/道/府)** | Administrative division. There are 47 in total |
| **City** | City where the main campus or headquarters is located (some universities have campuses in other cities) |
| **Famous Subjects** | Fields the university is widely known for. This is a general guide, not an official ranking |

## 📝 Notes and Corrections

| Item | Note |
|------|------|
| ⚠️ **Tokyo Tech and TMDU** | **Tokyo Institute of Technology** and **Tokyo Medical and Dental University** merged in October 2024 to form **Institute of Science Tokyo**. Their old websites redirect to the new one. They are kept as separate rows here to match your original list |
| **Wakayama University website** | The original list had a typo (`wakayma-u.ac.jp`). It is corrected here to `wakayama-u.ac.jp` |
| **"Public" vs "National"** | All universities in this list are **national universities (kokuritsu daigaku)**. In Japan, "public" universities (kōritsu) are run by prefectures or cities and are a separate group |
| **Multi-campus universities** | The city shown is the headquarters or main campus. Some universities have faculties in other cities (for example Shinshu, Yamaguchi, Shizuoka, and Gunma) |
| **Order** | The list is now sorted from north to south by Chihō, then by prefecture, and numbering has changed from the original |
| **Subjects** | The subject column is a general guide. Check each university's official website for current faculties, English-taught programs, and MEXT scholarship eligibility |
